# Rehear - Static 6-Month Vocal Training Curriculum

This document defines the default 6-month training plan built into the app.

The onboarding and voice check do not generate a completely new curriculum. Instead, they decide:

- where the user should start
- which weeks should be emphasized
- which weeks can be skipped or tested out
- which safety warnings or confidence supports should be applied
- how long the user's daily sessions should be

The curriculum itself remains stable, explainable, and easy to maintain.

---

## Product Promise

Rehear trains beginners through a 6-month daily vocal journey.

The goal is not to make users professional singers in six months. The realistic promise is:

> After six months, a beginner should understand their own voice better, practice safely, sing simple material more comfortably, and know how to improve a song through repeatable feedback.

The app should avoid overpromising. It should say "build a usable singing foundation," not "become a great singer."

---

## Curriculum Structure

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Main Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Weeks 1-4 | Breath and resonance foundation | User learns how to make sound more comfortably and steadily |
| Phase 2 | Weeks 5-12 | Vocal control and singing basics | User learns pitch, onset, endings, timing, and short melodic control |
| Phase 3 | Weeks 13-24 | Song application and performance | User applies technique to phrases, songs, range, expression, and full takes |

The first month is intentionally narrow. It focuses mostly on breath and resonance because beginners need a usable vocal setup before heavier song work.

---

## Weekly Session Pattern

Each week has one training theme. Daily sessions follow a consistent rhythm.

| Day | Role | What Happens |
|---:|---|---|
| Day 1 | Baseline | Record the week's core exercise and set a starting point |
| Day 2 | Stabilize | Repeat the easiest version with one cue |
| Day 3 | Clarify | Add one small technical detail |
| Day 4 | Retry | Repeat the same drill and compare to Day 1 |
| Day 5 | Apply | Use the skill in a tiny musical pattern |
| Day 6 | Review | Compare first and latest takes |
| Day 7 | Recovery / checkpoint | Gentle practice, reflection, or optional rest-aware session |

The app can offer one monthly grace day for missed practice without breaking the streak.

---

# Phase 1 - Weeks 1-4

## Breath and Resonance Foundation

The first month should not promise mastery. It should promise awareness, comfort, and a first layer of control.

User-facing framing:

> We are not starting with songs yet. We are building the voice that will sing them.

---

## Week 1 - Breath Awareness

**Goal:** Help the user feel steady outgoing air without pushing.

**Why it matters:** Many beginners treat singing as either holding breath or dumping breath. This week teaches a calmer middle path.

**Core exercises:**

- quiet inhale, soft hiss
- 5-second air release
- steady "sss" with no forced ending
- compare first hiss and second hiss

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Record a natural soft hiss |
| 2 | Smaller air | Repeat hiss with less force |
| 3 | Even ending | Keep the last third from collapsing |
| 4 | Relaxed inhale | Avoid shoulder lift and over-breathing |
| 5 | Timed release | 5-8 second steady hiss |
| 6 | Compare | First hiss vs latest hiss |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you release air without pushing?" |

**Feedback examples:**

- "The air faded near the end. Try using less air at the start."
- "This take was steadier because the beginning was smaller."

---

## Week 2 - Gentle Sound

**Goal:** Introduce sound without throat pressure.

**Why it matters:** Before pitch training, the user needs to create easy sound.

**Core exercises:**

- soft hum
- gentle "mm"
- hum for 3-5 seconds
- hum with comfort check

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline hum | Record a comfortable hum |
| 2 | Softer start | Begin the hum without a hard attack |
| 3 | Easy sustain | Hold a hum for 3-5 seconds |
| 4 | Tension check | Notice jaw, tongue, and throat pressure |
| 5 | Repeatability | Same hum twice, compare comfort |
| 6 | Hiss to hum | Air release, then hum |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you make a small sound without strain?" |

**Feedback examples:**

- "The start was a little abrupt. Try letting the hum arrive gently."
- "The second take sounded easier because you used less volume."

---

## Week 3 - Resonance Exploration

**Goal:** Help the user explore vibration and resonance without claiming exact anatomical diagnosis.

**Why it matters:** Beginners often push from the throat. Resonance exploration gives them another sensation to follow.

**Core exercises:**

- "mmm"
- "nnn"
- "ng"
- hum to vowel: "mmm-ah"
- front-of-face vibration exploration

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline resonance | Record a soft "mmm" |
| 2 | Find vibration | Notice lips/face vibration during hum |
| 3 | Keep it easy | Avoid pressing for more buzz |
| 4 | M to ah | Open from "mmm" to "ah" gently |
| 5 | Compare vowels | "mmm-ah" vs plain "ah" |
| 6 | Repeat best sound | Recreate the easier resonant take |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you find an easier, less throat-heavy sound?" |

**Feedback examples:**

- "The open vowel lost some steadiness. Try keeping the hum feeling as you open."
- "This sounded easier when the volume stayed small."

---

## Week 4 - Breath + Resonance Integration

**Goal:** Combine steady air, gentle sound, and resonance into short controlled tones.

**Why it matters:** This is the bridge from vocal setup into singing control.

**Core exercises:**

- hiss to hum
- hum to "ah"
- 3-5 second comfortable tone
- same tone retry

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline tone | Record hum to "ah" |
| 2 | Softer onset | Start the tone gently |
| 3 | Stable middle | Keep the tone from wobbling |
| 4 | Clean ending | Finish without dropping suddenly |
| 5 | Tiny musical move | "mm-ah" on one comfortable pitch |
| 6 | Month comparison | Week 1 sound vs Week 4 sound |
| 7 | Month 1 checkpoint | Decide whether to continue, repeat, or advance |

**Month 1 checkpoint:**

- Can the user release air steadily?
- Can the user hum without obvious strain?
- Can the user open from hum to vowel gently?
- Can the user hold a short sound for a few seconds?

Users who struggle should repeat selected Week 2-4 material. Users who do well can move into Phase 2.

---

# Phase 2 - Weeks 5-12

## Vocal Control and Singing Basics

This phase starts turning the prepared voice into singing behavior.

---

## Week 5 - Onset Control

**Goal:** Teach the user to start sound cleanly.

**Core exercises:**

- silent breath to hum
- soft "ah" start
- avoid hard glottal attack
- avoid breathy collapse

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline onset | Record a short "ah" |
| 2 | Softer start | Make the beginning less abrupt |
| 3 | Clearer start | Reduce excess breathiness |
| 4 | Repeat same start | Same sound twice |
| 5 | Hum to vowel start | Use hum as a bridge |
| 6 | Compare | First onset vs latest |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you begin sound without forcing it?" |

---

## Week 6 - Endings and Release

**Goal:** Keep phrase endings from fading, squeezing, or dropping.

**Core exercises:**

- 3-5 second tone endings
- gentle release
- sustained "ah"
- hum ending

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline ending | Record a short tone |
| 2 | Smaller beginning | Save air for the ending |
| 3 | Gentle release | Finish without squeezing |
| 4 | Endurance | Hold the same tone slightly longer |
| 5 | Apply | Two-syllable pattern like "ma-ma" |
| 6 | Compare | Ending stability |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can the ending stay alive?" |

---

## Week 7 - Single Pitch Matching

**Goal:** Match one comfortable reference pitch.

**Core exercises:**

- listen to note
- hum target
- open to vowel
- retry same note

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline match | Match one note |
| 2 | Hum first | Hum before opening |
| 3 | Reduce sliding | Land closer to target |
| 4 | Hold target | Stay on pitch briefly |
| 5 | Same note twice | Repeat exact target |
| 6 | Compare | First match vs latest |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you find one note more reliably?" |

---

## Week 8 - Pitch Stability

**Goal:** Keep pitch from drifting after the user finds it.

**Core exercises:**

- sustained target note
- stability bands
- shorter repeat attempts
- gentle correction

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline hold | Hold matched note |
| 2 | Smaller volume | Reduce drift by singing smaller |
| 3 | Middle stability | Keep the center steady |
| 4 | Ending stability | Avoid falling flat at end |
| 5 | Two attempts | Compare take 1 and take 2 |
| 6 | Review | Best hold of the week |
| 7 | Month 2 checkpoint | Pitch match and hold |

---

## Week 9 - Two-Note Movement

**Goal:** Move between two nearby notes without losing ease.

**Core exercises:**

- do-re echo
- hum two notes
- "ma-ma" step
- slow interval repeat

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline two-note echo | Echo two slow notes |
| 2 | Slow movement | Take more time between notes |
| 3 | Connect air | Avoid restarting each note |
| 4 | Same interval retry | Repeat exact pattern |
| 5 | Vowel version | Try on "ah" |
| 6 | Compare | First echo vs latest |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you move without grabbing?" |

---

## Week 10 - Three-Note Echo

**Goal:** Sing a tiny melodic shape.

**Core exercises:**

- three-note echo
- up/down pattern
- identify hardest note
- retry same shape

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline echo | Record three-note pattern |
| 2 | First note setup | Start cleanly |
| 3 | Middle note | Keep second note from sagging |
| 4 | Last note | Finish the shape |
| 5 | Slower version | Repeat at easier speed |
| 6 | Compare | Shape accuracy |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you echo a tiny melody?" |

---

## Week 11 - Timing and Rhythm Basics

**Goal:** Keep a simple vocal pattern in time.

**Core exercises:**

- clap/listen/sing
- two-beat pattern
- steady syllables
- no rushing

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline timing | Repeat a simple rhythm |
| 2 | Slow pulse | Sing with slower beat |
| 3 | Even syllables | Keep syllables equal |
| 4 | Avoid rushing | Compare early/late timing |
| 5 | Pitch + timing | Two notes in rhythm |
| 6 | Review | Best timed take |
| 7 | Checkpoint | "Can you wait for the beat?" |

---

## Week 12 - First Singing Phrase

**Goal:** Apply breath, resonance, pitch, and timing to a very short phrase.

**Core exercises:**

- 3-5 note phrase
- neutral syllable phrase
- simple lyric phrase
- before/after comparison

**Daily progression:**

| Day | Focus | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline phrase | Record a tiny phrase |
| 2 | Breath setup | Prepare before phrase |
| 3 | Pitch shape | Keep melody recognizable |
| 4 | Ending | Finish phrase cleanly |
| 5 | Retry phrase | Same phrase twice |
| 6 | Compare | Day 1 vs Day 6 |
| 7 | Month 3 checkpoint | Decide next song path |

---

# Phase 3 - Weeks 13-24

## Song Application and Performance

This phase makes the training feel like singing. It should still use short loops, not full-song overwhelm.

---

## Week 13 - Song Phrase Setup

**Goal:** Teach the user to prepare before a real phrase.

**Core exercises:**

- choose easy phrase
- hum phrase shape
- sing on neutral syllable
- sing with lyric

---

## Week 14 - Phrase Endings in Songs

**Goal:** Keep the ends of words and phrases alive.

**Core exercises:**

- last syllable sustain
- softer start for stronger ending
- phrase exit comparison

---

## Week 15 - Vowels and Tone Consistency

**Goal:** Keep tone from changing wildly between vowels.

**Core exercises:**

- ma-me-mi-mo-mu
- lyric vowel isolation
- hum to vowel

---

## Week 16 - Reference Fading

**Goal:** Reduce dependency on the app's guide tone.

**Core exercises:**

- listen and sing
- delayed reference
- sing first, check after
- compare guided vs unguided

**Month 4 checkpoint:** Can the user sing a short phrase with less guidance?

---

## Week 17 - Comfortable Range Expansion

**Goal:** Expand range carefully without pushing.

**Core exercises:**

- small upward patterns
- comfort check after each take
- transpose phrase lower or higher

**Safety rule:** If the user reports pain, stop range expansion and route to gentle material.

---

## Week 18 - Dynamics

**Goal:** Sing softer and louder without losing control.

**Core exercises:**

- soft-to-medium tone
- medium-to-soft phrase
- avoid shouting
- quiet singing that still carries

---

## Week 19 - Expression and Meaning

**Goal:** Add musical intention without sacrificing technique.

**Core exercises:**

- speak phrase
- hum phrase
- sing phrase with one emotional intention
- compare plain vs expressive take

---

## Week 20 - Consistency Across Takes

**Goal:** Make good takes repeatable.

**Core exercises:**

- same phrase three times
- identify best take
- repeat best setup
- compare consistency

**Month 5 checkpoint:** Are the user's good takes becoming less accidental?

---

## Week 21 - Verse Section

**Goal:** Train a longer but still manageable section.

**Core exercises:**

- split verse into two phrases
- train each phrase separately
- combine phrases
- compare section take

---

## Week 22 - Chorus or Peak Phrase

**Goal:** Handle the most demanding phrase without pushing.

**Core exercises:**

- identify peak note
- sing softer through peak
- transpose if needed
- repeat with comfort check

---

## Week 23 - Full Short Song Take

**Goal:** Record a short full take or a complete selected section.

**Core exercises:**

- warm-up
- phrase reminders
- full take
- one fix
- second full take

---

## Week 24 - Final Comparison and Next Plan

**Goal:** Compare the user's earliest recordings to their current singing and recommend the next path.

**Core exercises:**

- replay Month 1 baseline
- replay Month 6 phrase
- record final selected song section
- choose next focus: pitch, range, expression, confidence, or song library

**Month 6 checkpoint:**

- What changed in breath?
- What changed in sound comfort?
- What changed in pitch/melody?
- What changed in confidence?
- What should the user train next?

---

## Phase 3 Weekly Detail

Weeks 13-24 can use the same 7-day structure:

| Day | Role | Session |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Record phrase or song section |
| 2 | Isolate | Train the hardest small piece |
| 3 | Technique cue | Apply one cue from earlier phases |
| 4 | Retry | Same phrase, same cue |
| 5 | Musical apply | Add lyric, expression, or dynamics |
| 6 | Compare | First take vs best take |
| 7 | Checkpoint | Save clip, choose next phrase |

---

## Placement Rules

Onboarding and voice check should place the user into this fixed curriculum.

| User State | Recommended Start |
|---|---|
| Total beginner, nervous, or unclear issues | Week 1 |
| Breath fades or voice feels thin | Week 1 or Week 2 |
| Can hum comfortably but pitch is weak | Week 5 or Week 7 |
| Good musical ear but weak voice control | Week 5 with accelerated checks |
| Strain, tightness, or pain | Week 2 with safety restrictions |
| Already sings songs comfortably | Week 12 or Week 13 placement check |

The app should always allow earlier weeks to be opened voluntarily.

---

## Skip and Test-Out Rules

Users should not feel trapped in beginner material.

Offer three controls:

1. **Follow recommended path**
2. **Take a skip-ahead check**
3. **Review earlier foundation**

A test-out check should require:

- one baseline recording
- one retry after feedback
- no pain or strain report
- enough stability to handle the next phase

The app should phrase this gently:

> You can move ahead, but we recommend keeping one foundation drill in your weekly routine.

---

## Streak and Grace Day Rule

Rehear should encourage daily discipline while allowing real life.

Rules:

- Each calendar month includes 1 grace day.
- The first missed day in a month does not break the streak.
- The second missed day breaks the streak.
- The 180-day journey never resets.
- The app should distinguish between "streak" and "journey progress."

Example copy:

> You used your monthly grace day. Your streak is safe. Come back tomorrow.

> Your streak reset, but your journey did not. Start again today.

---

## Daily Notification Examples

Notifications should include the current journey context.

| Tone | Example |
|---|---|
| Gentle | "Day 12 of 180. A few steady minutes are enough today." |
| Strict | "Training time. Keep the promise you made to your voice." |
| Coach-like | "Show up, record once, improve one thing." |
| Motivational | "Six months is built one honest session at a time." |

Phase-specific examples:

- Week 1: "Today is breath work. Keep it small and steady."
- Week 3: "Find the hum before the vowel."
- Week 7: "One note, one retry. Pitch grows through repetition."
- Week 14: "Phrase endings matter. Finish the sound gently."
- Week 23: "Today you record the full section, then improve one thing."

## Implementation Notes

The curriculum can be represented as structured data.

Each week should include:

- `phase`
- `weekNumber`
- `title`
- `goal`
- `coreExercises`
- `dailySessions`
- `checkpoint`
- `tags`
- `difficulty`
- `skipRules`
- `safetyRules`

Example:

```ts
{
  phase: "foundation",
  weekNumber: 3,
  title: "Resonance Exploration",
  goal: "Explore easier vibration and reduce throat-heavy sound.",
  tags: ["resonance", "hum", "gentle", "foundation"],
  difficulty: 1,
  canTestOut: true,
  safetyRules: ["stop_if_pain", "avoid_loud_volume"]
}
```

---

## Summary

The fixed 6-month Rehear curriculum should be:

1. Narrow and serious in Month 1: breath and resonance only.
2. Skill-building in Months 2-3: onset, endings, pitch, timing, short phrases.
3. Musical in Months 4-6: song phrases, range, dynamics, expression, full takes.
4. Flexible at the edges: users can test out, repeat, or review.
5. Always built around the Rehear loop: record, observe, fix one thing, retry.
