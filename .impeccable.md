## Design Context

### Users
VoiceFix is for beginner and returning singers who feel that "something sounds off" but do not know what to practice next. They may use YouTube, tuner apps, karaoke, or occasional teacher advice, and they often feel embarrassed hearing their recorded voice. The app should help them get a clear, personal next step through short guided recordings and comparison against their own previous takes.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and encouraging. VoiceFix should feel like a trustworthy practice companion: emotionally safe, technically credible, and direct enough to help users act without drowning them in vocal jargon.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first, refined, and instrument-like rather than game-like. Use warm off-white surfaces, dark ink text, focused accent colors, restrained data visuals, and generous spacing. Avoid karaoke visuals, neon AI gradients, dancing mascots, fake medical precision, and school-like lesson walls.

### Design Principles
- Personalization should feel earned: ask enough useful questions to make the plan feel specific, but explain each step through plain outcomes.
- Every onboarding screen should reduce uncertainty or shame: privacy, comparison-to-self, and optional retry matter.
- Show the roadmap as a personalized plan, not a forced month-long lockout.
- Use one dominant action per screen and make secondary exits respectful.
- Prefer careful language: "sounds like," "likely," and "try this" over absolute diagnosis.
- Keep every feature simple and straightforward. Avoid crude, overbuilt, or decorative complexity; make the next action obvious before adding polish.
