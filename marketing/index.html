Calm by design

Private practice. Careful claims.

Voice feedback should help you act, not make you feel graded or diagnosed. Rehear uses cautious language and keeps safety boundaries visible.

Your saved practice recordings and progress stay on your device.
Feedback is a practice suggestion, not medical advice.
If singing hurts, stop and consider a qualified professional.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Rehear a vocal teacher?

No. Rehear is a practice companion for short feedback-and-retry loops. It can supplement lessons, but it does not replace a qualified teacher or clinician.

Does it score my voice?

No public ranking or talent score. Rehear looks for useful changes between your own takes and describes them in plain language.

What happens to my recordings?

Saved practice clips remain on your device. A temporary copy is sent for analysis when you request feedback and is not intentionally retained after the request finishes. See the Privacy Policy for details.

When will Rehear be available?

Rehear is preparing for early access on iPhone, with Android support planned. Email us to join the first group of testers.
